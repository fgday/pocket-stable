My Lovely Pony is a little stable in your pocket. Every pony you welcome is one of a kind: its own coat, mane, eyes and name, unlike any other.

Your ponies live in real time. They get hungry while you're away, doze off at night, and are glad to see you when you come back.

Look after them

  • Feed them carrots when they're hungry, but not too many… an overfed pony may not stay a pony for long!
  • Wash them, clean up after them, and stroke them until they're happy.
  • Ask how they feel: your pony will find a way to tell you, in its own words.
  • A gentle reminder lets you know when someone is getting hungry.

Watch them grow

  • Every pony starts as a wobbly foal, grows into a yearling, and becomes an adult.
  • Day turns to night over the paddock, and every pony keeps its own bedtime.

  2. Yearling
  3. Grown

Build your stable

The better you care for your ponies, the more your stable grows: climb the ranks, celebrate every level, and unlock room for new ponies.

Every pony is one of a kind

A real bond

Your ponies depend on you. Forget one for too long and it will leave the stable for good, so pop in often, even just for a cuddle.

No account required, no connection needed: your stable lives on your phone.
