Privacy policy

My Lovely Pony - Virtual Pet · Effective 30 September 2026

My Lovely Pony - Virtual Pet is made by foggy.day (“we” on this page).

In short

My Lovely Pony doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. Your stable stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It never connects to the internet, and doesn't even ask Android for permission to.

Your stable

Your ponies and your progress are saved only on your device, and we never see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules its reminders on your device. Nothing is sent to or from a server.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include your saved game in your device's backup to your Google account. That backup is a Google service, governed by your account settings and Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive it.

Google Play

You download the app from Google Play, which may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We only see the aggregated statistics Google Play gives every developer, such as install counts, which don't identify you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes any of this, we'll update this page before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest change.

Contact

Questions? Write to info@foggy.day.
